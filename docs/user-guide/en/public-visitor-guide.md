# Public Visitor Guide

**Audience:** anyone visiting the Smart Computing Lab website without an account.
**Purpose:** find your way around the public site — research, people, publications, news, events and more — with no login required.
**Prerequisites:** none. A web browser and the site's URL are all you need.

## What's on the site

The header groups everything into three menus, plus two standalone links:

- **Research** — Research Areas, Projects, Publications, Knowledge, Resources
- **People** — Team, Groups
- **Community** — Forum, News, Events, Gallery
- **Contact** — a standalone link
- **Log in** — top right, for lab members

Some content is marked **lab-only** by its owners and is not shown to a public visitor; everything described below is what guests can actually see. A **language switcher** in the header toggles the whole site between English and Japanese at any time — it does not reload the page or lose your place.

## Home

The landing page (`/`) gives a quick overview: a short lab introduction, highlighted research areas, a handful of recent news items, and quick links into the rest of the site. It's the fastest way to get oriented before diving into a specific section.

## Research Areas

`/research` lists the lab's broad research themes (for example, computer vision or FPGA/VLSI design), each with a short description. Click one (`/research/:id`) to see its detail page: a longer description plus the projects, researchers, publications, news and events connected to that area — each filtered to what is publicly visible.

## Projects

`/projects` lists concrete research projects, each showing its status (planned, active, completed, or archived) and the research group running it. A project's own page (`/projects/:id`) lists its members, the research areas and publications it is linked to, and any public events tied to it.

## Team

`/team` ("Our Team") is the lab's people page. Its sections appear in this order, each with a count: **Faculty**, **PhD Students**, **MSc Students**, **BSc Students**, **Research Students** and, last, **Alumni**. Every section uses the same profile card: a photo (or initials), name, role, department and any research profile links. Click a name (`/team/:id`) for that person's public profile — bio, history, and the publications, news, projects, groups and events they're connected to, all filtered to what is public.

### Alumni

**Alumni** — former members who have agreed to be listed — is the final section of the Team page, directly after Research Students. There is no separate Alumni page or menu item. The old `/alumni` address simply takes you to the Alumni section of the Team page (`/team#alumni`). A person appears there only after a lab administrator has published their profile, so if no alumni have been published yet, the Alumni section is not shown at all. No login is needed to see published alumni.

## Groups

`/groups` lists the lab's research groups; each group's page (`/groups/:id`) shows its description and its public projects and members.

## Publications

`/publications` is a filterable, sortable list of the lab's papers (by year, with search). Each publication's page (`/publications/:id`) shows its full author list (linking to team profiles where available), venue, year, and any available links (PDF, DOI). Where the lab has recorded them, a **Publication details** list also shows the status (Published, Accepted or Under submission), paper category, journal list number, volume, pages, publication month and date of acceptance; anything that is not stated is simply not shown. A paper that is only accepted or under submission is not presented as published.

## News

`/news` lists lab announcements and updates — awards, accepted papers, open positions and similar — newest first, each optionally linked to the project it's about.

## Events

`/events` lists upcoming and past lab events (seminars, meetings, deadlines, social events), each showing its date, time, location and kind. An event's own page (`/events/:id`) shows its full description and any related project.

## Gallery

`/gallery`, where visible, shows photos from lab life, events and research, organized by category.

## Knowledge

`/knowledge` lists the lab's public documentation — methods notes, dataset documentation, procedures — that the lab has chosen to publish. A document's page (`/knowledge/:id`) shows its full body text.

## Resources

`/resources` lists the lab's public research infrastructure — datasets, hardware, software, tools and similar — with details on each (`/resources/:id`): version, vendor, identifier, environment notes and links to related projects, publications, events or knowledge documents.

## Community Forum

`/community/forum` shows forum categories that have been made public. A public category's topics and comments are readable without an account; posting, commenting and reacting all require a login (see the Researcher Guide).

## Search

`/search` searches across the site's public content — projects, publications, team members, news, research areas and more — in one place.

## Contact

`/contact` shows the lab's location (Interdisciplinary Faculty of Science and Engineering, Matsue Campus, Shimane University), email address, office hours and the university website. **The message form on that page is not connected to an email service — nothing you type into it is sent.** Write to the email address shown on the page instead.

## What you can and cannot do without an account

| You can (no login) | You cannot |
|---|---|
| Browse the home page, research areas, projects, groups, team and published alumni, publications, news, events, gallery, knowledge, resources and public forum categories | See anything marked lab-only |
| Search public content and switch between English and Japanese | Post, comment or react in the forum |
| Read the Quick Start, Public Visitor and Researcher Onboarding guides under **Help & Documentation** (`/docs`) | Create or edit any content, message members, or see unpublished alumni |
| Open an invitation link you were sent (`/invite/...`) | Register yourself — accounts are created only by an administrator |

## Signing in

The **Log in** link in the header is for existing lab accounts only — see the Researcher Guide. This site does not offer public self-registration; a lab administrator creates every account (see the Administrator Guide).

## Common questions

- **I don't see a page I expected.** It may be marked lab-only by its owner, or it may not be published yet — this is intentional, not a bug.
- **The page is in the wrong language.** Use the language switcher in the header; your choice is remembered on this device.
- **Can I create my own account?** Not directly — contact the lab (see Contact) and ask an administrator to invite you.
