# Quick Start Guide

**Audience:** anyone new to the site — visitor or newly-invited member.
**Purpose:** get oriented in about five minutes.
**Prerequisites:** none.

## 1. Access the website

Open the site's URL in any browser. No account is needed to browse.

## 2. Activate your account (invited members only)

If a lab administrator has invited you, you received a private link (`/invite/<token>`). Open it, confirm it shows your email, and choose your own password — no one else, including the administrator, will ever see it. This activates your account and logs you in immediately. (No invitation yet? See Contact below.)

## 3. Log in

Click **Log in** in the header, enter your email and password. **Log out** is in the Account menu once signed in.

## 4. Complete your profile

Once signed in, open **Account → My Profile** (`/profile`). Fill in your name, role/title, department, a short bio, and optionally a photo URL. This is what your public team page shows.

## 5. Find research

Use the **Research** menu for Research Areas, Projects, Publications, Knowledge documents and lab Resources.

## 6. Find researchers

Use the **People** menu for the Team roster and Research Groups. Click any name for their full public profile.

## 7. Find publications & projects

`/publications` and `/projects` each support filtering and search; a Research Area's own page also rolls up everything connected to it.

## 8. Use member features (once logged in)

- **Workspace** — your own projects, groups and research areas in one place.
- **Messages** / **Notifications** — private conversations and activity addressed to you.
- **Community → Forum** — post, comment and react in categories you can see.
- Add publications, news, research areas, events, knowledge docs, resources and gallery items — all start as lab-only until a manager publishes them.

## 9. Get help

See the fuller **Public Visitor Guide** or **Researcher/Lab Member Guide**, or use **Contact** in the header to reach the lab directly.

## 10. Log out

Account menu → **Log out**, from any page.
